FAQ

Common questions

Is ReelSynth free?

Yes. Free to download, free to use, MIT licensed. No account, no trial, no limits.

Does it work in Ableton?

Yes. ReelSynth ships as a VST3/AU plugin you can load on any MIDI track in Ableton Live.

What about other DAWs?

The VST3 plugin works in any DAW that supports VST3. AU is available for Logic and other AU hosts.

Can I import my Vital/Serum tables?

Yes. ReelSynth reads .vitaltable, Serum .fxp, and single-cycle WAV folders.

Windows, Mac, and Linux?

All three. Grab the latest build for your platform from the download section above.

Does it work with ReelStudio?

Yes. ReelSynth is compatible with ReelStudio for offline rendering and patch export into studio pipelines.
